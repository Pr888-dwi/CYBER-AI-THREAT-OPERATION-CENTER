# Research Paper Outline

## Title
CyberAI: An AI-Powered Cybersecurity Platform for Intrusion Detection, Phishing Analysis, Malware Scanning, and SOC Automation

## Abstract
This paper presents CyberAI, a unified AI-driven cybersecurity platform that integrates machine learning and generative AI for intrusion detection, phishing detection, malware analysis, and SOC support. The proposed system aims to reduce analyst workload while improving detection speed and explainability.

## Keywords
Cybersecurity, Intrusion Detection, Phishing Detection, Malware Analysis, Generative AI, SOC Automation
